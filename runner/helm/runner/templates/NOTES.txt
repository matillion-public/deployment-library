WARNING: terminationGracePeriodSeconds is {{ $grace }}s, below the runner's own
shutdown timeout of 43200s (12h). A runner pod uses that window to finish the
pipeline tasks it is already running. At {{ $grace }}s, a node drain, cluster
upgrade or rolling update will SIGKILL runners that are still working, and those
tasks fail rather than drain — a planned failover becomes a lossy one.

Set dpcAgent.dpcAgent.gracePeriodSeconds back to 43200 unless losing in-flight
tasks on every voluntary disruption is a deliberate, accepted trade-off.
